Our Story

A Legacy of
Unparalleled Luxury

Nestled in the heart of the city, LUMIÈRE was born from a singular obsession: to create spaces where beauty and comfort are inseparable. Every detail — from hand-stitched linens to curated art — reflects our devotion to the extraordinary.

Our 200 rooms and suites are sanctuaries of calm, our kitchens led by Michelin-starred talent, and our staff trained in the rare art of anticipating desire before it is spoken.

The LUMIÈRE Experience

Curated for the
Discerning Guest

Fine Dining

Two Michelin-starred cuisine crafted from locally-sourced seasonal ingredients. An evening at LUMIÈRE's restaurant is a journey in itself.

Spa & Wellness

A 3,000 sq ft sanctuary offering ancient healing rituals, bespoke therapies, and restorative treatments curated by world-class therapists.

Rooftop Pool

Infinity-edge rooftop pool with panoramic city views. Sunbeds, cocktails, and the sky stretching endlessly — the perfect urban escape.

Event Spaces

From intimate gatherings to grand celebrations — our versatile event spaces accommodate up to 800 guests with state-of-the-art AV facilities.

24hr Concierge

Our dedicated concierge team is available around the clock to arrange transfers, reservations, private tours, and the seemingly impossible.

Luxury Suites

Each suite is a private world — panoramic windows, Italian marble baths, and curated art collections that redefine what it means to feel at home.
